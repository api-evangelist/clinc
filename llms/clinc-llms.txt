# Clinc

> Clinc is a conversational AI platform for banking and financial services, delivering Natural Language Understanding (NLU), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Natural Language Generation (NLG) so institutions can build virtual assistants and chatbots. Founded 2015 in Ann Arbor, Michigan; deployed with customers including USAA, Ford, Barclays, and İşbank; backed by Insight Partners, Drive Capital, and eLab Ventures.
